How to Find WRDW+

Follow the steps below to watch WRDW+ on your television. Apple TV users should search for WRDW News 12. Android TV or Google TV users can try searching WRDW or WRDW News.

Watch On
Apple TV
  • Step 1: Open the App Store on Apple TV.
  • Step 2: Go to Search.
  • Step 3: Type WRDW News 12.
  • Step 4: Download the app and open it to watch WRDW+.
Search for WRDW News 12, not just WRDW+.
Watch On
Android TV
  • Step 1: Open Google Play on your TV.
  • Step 2: Search WRDW or WRDW News.
  • Step 3: If WRDW appears, install and open it.
  • Step 4: If it does not appear, watch WRDW+ on Fire TV or Roku instead.
Android TV availability may vary by device and store listing.
